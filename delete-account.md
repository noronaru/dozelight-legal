# Delete your Dozelight account

Dozelight is a sleep tracker by **Noronaru**. You only have a Dozelight account if you signed in with Google in **Settings → Account**. If you never did, we hold no data about you: everything is on your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

**One account for Dozelight, Hydrorep and InnerWeather.** Our apps sign in with the same Google account and share one account. Deleting it in any of them deletes it for all of them, including InnerWeather's cloud backup of your journal and the summaries Hydrorep shares.

## Delete it in the app

1. Open Dozelight and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.
3. Choose your Google account once more to confirm it's you.

You need an internet connection. Your account and its data are deleted right away.

## Delete it without the app

Email **noronaru@proton.me** from the email address of your Google account, with the subject **Delete my account**. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We delete your account and its data within **30 days** and let you know when it's done.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account: the email address, name and account identifier Google sign-in gave us
- Every night summary Dozelight shared with Hydrorep and InnerWeather
- Everything Hydrorep and InnerWeather keep in the same account, such as InnerWeather's cloud backup of your journal

## What is kept

- **What's on your phone.** Your nights, notes, tags and Health Connect readings were never on our servers, so they stay on your phone. To delete them, use **Settings → Your data → Delete all data** or uninstall the app.
- **Files you exported**, wherever you saved them.
- **Anonymous crash reports**, which contain no account or sleep data and are deleted by Sentry after its retention period.
- **Purchases** stay on record with Google Play under its own terms. If you bought something in Dozelight, email us to also delete the purchase record held by RevenueCat.

We don't keep a copy of deleted data. Google, which hosts our database, may take some time to clear it from its own backup systems.
